# Boreal & northern — indicator species

**Climate signal:** these species indicate **Köppen Dfc · Dfb · ET** — subarctic / cold-continental & tundra edge. See the [Köppen-Geiger map & quick read](/wiki/identify/vegetation-climate/) to place the zone.

## Silver birch (*Betula pendula*)

![Silver birch](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Betula_pendula_Finland.jpg?width=640)
{ width=340 }

**Geolocation tell:** THE Russia/Scandinavia/Baltics tree; white bark with black fissures

Betula pendula, commonly known as silver birch, warty birch, European white birch, or East Asian white birch, is a species of tree in the family Betulaceae, native to Europe and parts of Asia, though in southern Europe, it is only found at higher altitudes. Its range extends into Siberia, China, and southwest Asia in the mountains of northern Turkey, the Caucasus, and northern Iran. It has been introduced into North America, where it is known as the European white birch or weeping birch and is considered invasive in some states in the United States and parts of Canada. *([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betula_pendula), CC BY-SA)*

## Scots pine (*Pinus sylvestris*)

![Scots pine](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Skuleskogen_pine.jpg?width=640)
{ width=340 }

**Geolocation tell:** Orange upper bark; Eurasia's most widespread pine

Pinus sylvestris is a species of tree in the pine family Pinaceae that is native to Eurasia. It is commonly known as the Scots pine in English; it is also known as the Scotch pine in the United States, and occasionally called the Baltic pine or European red pine. It can readily be identified by its combination of fairly short, blue-green leaves and orange-red bark. *([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinus_sylvestris), CC BY-SA)*

## Siberian larch (*Larix sibirica*)

![Siberian larch](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Siberian-larch.jpg?width=640)
{ width=340 }

**Geolocation tell:** Deciduous conifer — golden in autumn, bare in winter = Siberia/Mongolia

Larix sibirica, the Siberian larch or Russian larch, is a frost-hardy tree native to western Russia, from close to the Finnish border east to the Yenisei valley in central Siberia, where it hybridises with the Dahurian larch L. gmelinii of eastern Siberia; the hybrid is known as Larix × czekanowskii. *([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larix_sibirica), CC BY-SA)*
