# Flags

Flags appear constantly in street imagery — on public buildings, schools, car stickers,
license plates and advertising — and are readable at distances where text isn't.
Authoritative references: [CIA World Factbook flag pages](https://www.cia.gov/the-world-factbook/field/flag-description/)
and the [Wikipedia gallery of sovereign-state flags](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gallery_of_sovereign_state_flags)
(every flag with construction details, sourced to national law).[^refs]

## Look-alike groups (the usual traps)

| Group | Tell them apart |
|---|---|
| **Netherlands / Luxembourg / Russia / Paraguay** | LU's blue is lighter than NL; RU stripes white-blue-red (blue centre); PY has a central emblem |
| **Indonesia / Monaco / Poland** | ID and MC nearly identical red-white (MC shorter ratio); PL is white-red (inverted) |
| **Romania / Chad / Moldova / Andorra** | RO-TD almost identical blue-yellow-red; MD and AD carry coats of arms |
| **Ireland / Côte d'Ivoire / Italy** | IE green-white-orange; CI orange-white-green (reversed); IT green-white-red |
| **Nordic crosses** | DK red/white; SE blue/yellow; NO red/white/blue; FI white/blue; IS blue/white/red |
| **Slavic tricolours** | SI, SK, RS, HR all white-blue-red variants — the **coat of arms** is the tell |
| **Australia / New Zealand** | AU: 6 white stars + Commonwealth star; NZ: 4 red stars |
| **Central America blues** | GT, HN, SV, NI all blue-white-blue — emblem differs |
| **Pan-African / Pan-Arab** | Ethiopia-derived green-yellow-red vs black-white-green-red sets — count stripes & emblems |

## Sub-national flags localize further

Regional flags pin a photo *inside* a country: Swiss cantons, German Länder, Spanish autonomías
(Catalan senyera!), US state flags, Canadian provinces — plus dependencies (Greenland, Faroes,
Gibraltar…). See the locally-cached galleries:
**[Subnational & dependency flags](flags-subnational/index.md)**.

[^refs]: CIA World Factbook, [Flag descriptions](https://www.cia.gov/the-world-factbook/field/flag-description/); Wikipedia, [Gallery of sovereign state flags](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gallery_of_sovereign_state_flags); Smith, W., *Flags Through the Ages and Across the World* (McGraw-Hill) — the standard vexillology reference.
