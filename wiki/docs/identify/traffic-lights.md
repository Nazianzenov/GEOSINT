# Traffic lights

Signal housings, backplates and mounting styles are **nationally standardized** — a traffic
light in frame is a country filter. Standards: Vienna Convention on Road Signs and Signals
(signal chapter) for most of Eurasia; the US MUTCD Part 4 for the Americas-influenced
systems.[^std]

## Fast filters

| Feature | Where |
|---|---|
| **Horizontal** signal heads | USA (many states), Japan, South Korea, Mexico, much of Latin America, Philippines |
| **Vertical** heads | Europe, Russia/CIS, Australia/NZ, most of Africa & Middle East |
| **Yellow/black striped backplates** | Canada (very common), parts of US, Israel, Argentina |
| **White-bordered black backplates** | UK, Ireland, Australia |
| **Countdown timers integrated** | SE Asia (VN/TH/ID), China, Latin America, Russia (newer) |
| **Signals on far side of junction** | North America standard; near-side = most of Europe[^place] |
| **Small repeater light at eye level** | Germany/Austria/CEE (for the first car at the line) |
| **Bi-colour single lamp flashing** | Pedestrian variants differ: red man/green man figures are nationally drawn (Ampelmännchen = ex-East Germany!) |

## Mounting

- **Span-wire (hanging on cables)**: classic US/Canada look, also Japan.
- **Mast arms over the junction**: US, AU, newer European arterials.
- **Simple posts at the corner**: dense European cities, Russia/CIS.
