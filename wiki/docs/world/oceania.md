# Oceania — recognition notes

All major countries drive **left**.

## Australia

- White flexible guide posts with **red reflector on the left** of the road[^austroads];
  black-on-yellow warning diamonds (MUTCD-derived); "GIVE WAY" text signs.
- State plates: NSW black-on-yellow has been replaced by white, but state slogans persist —
  see [Wikipedia AU plates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Australia).
- Landscape: eucalypt bush, red centre, Queenslander stilt houses (QLD), Colorbond roofs.
- "Big things" roadside statues; servo brands (BP/Caltex/United); school zone flashing signs.

## New Zealand

- Yellow no-passing centre lines paired with white; "LSZ" limited speed zones (historic);
  NZTA chevron boards[^nzta].
- Landscape: tussock high country (South Is.), tree ferns + pōhutukawa (North Is.), sheep
  density, single-lane bridges with priority signs — a strong NZ tell.
- Māori place names (wh-, -nui, -roa) on green signage.

## Pacific Islands

- **Papua New Guinea**: Tok Pisin signage ("haus"), highlands vs coastal split; limited SV.
- **Fiji** (LHT): sugar-cane fields, Fiji Indian temples + mosques alongside churches.
- **Samoa** (LHT since 2009[^samoa]): open fale houses without walls — unique.
- **Vanuatu (RHT!)** vs neighbours — one of the Pacific's only RHT (French colonial legacy
  shared with New Caledonia/French Polynesia, all RHT with French signage).
- **New Caledonia/French Polynesia**: French plates & signage in the tropics.

## Complete coverage — the remaining islands

| Country/territory | Drives | Recognition |
|---|---|---|
| **Tonga** | Left | Royal palace in Nukuʻalofa; churches everywhere, everything closed Sundays |
| **Solomon Islands** | Left | Honiara; WWII relics; betel-nut stalls |
| **Kiribati** | Left | Atolls straddling the equator/date line; causeway-linked Tarawa |
| **Tuvalu / Nauru** | Left | Tiny: Funafuti's airstrip doubles as public space; Nauru's phosphate moonscape |
| **Marshall Is. / Palau / FSM** | Right | US-influenced (plates, signs, dollars) — the RHT+US look separates them from LHT neighbours |
| **Guam / N. Marianas / American Samoa** | Right | US territories: full US signage & plates in the tropics (Am. Samoa drives right despite Samoa driving left!) |
| **New Caledonia / French Polynesia / Wallis & Futuna** | Right | France overseas: French plates, euro/XPF, baguettes in the tropics |
| **Cook Islands / Niue** | Left | NZ-associated: NZ-style signage |

[^austroads]: Austroads, *Guide to Road Design Part 6B* — delineation; state implementations (e.g. TMR QLD, VicRoads).
[^nzta]: Waka Kotahi NZTA, [Traffic Control Devices Manual](https://www.nzta.govt.nz/resources/traffic-control-devices-manual/).
[^samoa]: BBC News, [Samoa switches to driving on left](http://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/asia-pacific/8240992.stm) (7 Sep 2009).
