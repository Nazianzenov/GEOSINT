# South America — recognition notes

All RHT except Guyana & Suriname (LHT). Guides: [PlonkIt](https://www.plonkit.net/guide).[^plonkit]

- **Brazil**: Portuguese (ã/õ) — the only Portuguese-speaking country on the continent;
  Mercosur white-blue plates; red ceramic-block construction; VW Gol/Fiat density; "Lotérica"
  and "Açaí" signage; biome range from Amazon to pampa.
- **Argentina**: black-on-white→Mercosur plates; "YPF" fuel stations; Fernet/mate culture
  ads; poplar windbreaks in the pampa; ñ + voseo Spanish ("vos").
- **Chile**: the Andes wall to the east (or west shadows in afternoon!); "COPEC" stations;
  Atacama hyper-aridity north, temperate rainforest south; orange-reflector bollards similar
  to Iberia.
- **Uruguay**: Montevideo rambla; ceibo trees; Mercosur plates; very European feel.
- **Peru**: "mototaxis"; adobe + unfinished brick; Inca terracing in highlands; yellow taxis
  (Lima).
- **Bolivia**: cholitas' bowler hats; El Alto brick texture; altiplano light.
- **Ecuador**: equatorial sun (shadows flip seasonally!); yellow taxis; Andes+coast+Amazon
  in a small area.
- **Colombia**: yellow plates (front+rear) — strong tell[^co]; "Drogas/Drogueria" pharmacies;
  guadua bamboo in coffee zone.
- **Paraguay**: Guaraní bilingual signage; red soil; terere culture.
- **Venezuela**: limited recent coverage; old US car fleet; "Bodega" signage.
- **Guyana/Suriname** (LHT): English vs Dutch signage respectively — unique on the continent;
  stilt houses, tropical coastal plain.

## Territories & edge cases

| Territory | Recognition |
|---|---|
| **French Guiana** | **France/EU in South America**: French plates, euro, gendarmerie, Ariane launch site at Kourou |
| **Falkland Islands** (LHT) | UK overseas: drives left, red phone boxes, treeless moorland, peat |
| **Galápagos** (EC) | Strict signage, sea lions on benches; Ecuadorian plates |
| **Easter Island** (CL) | Moai; Chilean plates 3,700 km from the mainland |

[^plonkit]: PlonkIt country pages, [plonkit.net/guide](https://www.plonkit.net/guide).
[^co]: Colombia's Resolución 12379 vehicle plate standard (yellow private plates).
