Two-shadow mode — a second reading taken a known time later

Two shadows of the same scene at a known interval apart pin the time far more tightly (a single reading often leaves two windows). Enter the second reading and the minutes between them.

Species distribution overlay (GBIF)

Live occurrence-density tiles for any species — e.g. Betula pendula (silver birch) to compare a scene's vegetation against the real range. See the wiki's botanical reference for one-click species maps.

OSM data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL) via Overpass; species tiles: GBIF occurrence density (CC0/CC-BY).

Offline tile prefetch (cache this area)

Download every map tile for the current view across a zoom range into the local cache, so the area works fully offline later (fieldwork OPSEC).

Tiles cached 30 days in cache/tiles/. A large area or high max-zoom is capped (6000 tiles) — zoom in or lower the depth if it refuses.
